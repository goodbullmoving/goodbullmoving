Ask for an estimate ↗
Does GoodBull provide the truck?

Not for our current labor-only offering. You rent and arrange the truck, trailer, or container; our crew provides loading, unloading, and on-site moving labor. GoodBull does not transport household goods under this offering.

How much does labor-only moving cost?

Our standard two-person labor rate is $85 per hour with a two-hour minimum ($170). Stair charges and other job-specific fees may apply; the final terms are confirmed before booking.

Can you load PODS and U-Box containers?

Yes. We help customers load and unload customer-arranged portable storage containers, subject to scheduling and job details.

Do you help with apartment stairs?

Yes. Tell us how many floors, whether an elevator is available, and any difficult furniture. Our standard stair fee is generally $25 per charged floor.

Can you handle safes, pianos, or jacuzzis?

We review heavy and specialty items individually. Send measurements, photos, estimated weight, and details about access before we confirm whether we can safely accommodate the job.

Is the online estimate a final quote?

No. It is a preliminary estimate based on your selected hours and standard stair charges. We will confirm charges, timing, and availability directly before scheduling.
